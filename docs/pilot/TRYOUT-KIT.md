# LearnSprint: first usability and scenario review

Status: prepared September 27, 2026. No recruited participants or results yet.

## Purpose

Find where people cannot understand or complete the shift, and whether its constraints resemble entry-level operations work. This small convenience sample cannot establish learning gains, job readiness or market demand.

## Participants and session

Recruit 3–5 adults preparing for a first operations/order-support role, plus one practitioner with order handling or dispatch experience. Participation is optional; stopping has no consequences. Ask permission before recording; default to anonymous written notes. Use P01–P05, not names. No CV, employer/customer records or real order data. Keep raw notes private and publish only consented, anonymized summaries. Delete raw recordings after analysis within 30 days unless separately agreed.

Use https://d2g4a2ezl5lw7r.cloudfront.net/career in a browser not used for earlier practice. Allow 15 minutes for the shift and 5 for feedback. Record actual completion time; do not impose it as a pass/fail score. If unfinished, note the stopping point. English proficiency is not assessed; the facilitator may translate the prompt without explaining the solution.

## Participant prompt (English)

You are covering the order desk at a fictional stationery supplier. Read the customer promises and available information. Make a plan you would be willing to hand over. When circumstances change, decide what to do next and save a handoff. You may ask the contacts questions or request guidance. Please say what you expect to happen before pressing an unfamiliar control. We are evaluating the product, not you.

## Lời hướng dẫn (Tiếng Việt)

Bạn đang phụ trách bàn điều phối đơn hàng của một nhà cung cấp văn phòng phẩm giả định. Đọc cam kết với khách và các thông tin có sẵn, rồi lập phương án mà bạn sẵn sàng bàn giao. Khi tình huống thay đổi, quyết định bước tiếp theo và lưu bàn giao. Bạn có thể hỏi các nhân vật hoặc xin hướng dẫn. Khi gặp nút chưa rõ, hãy nói bạn nghĩ điều gì sẽ xảy ra trước khi bấm. Chúng tôi đang đánh giá sản phẩm, không đánh giá bạn.

## Moderator instructions

Do not give allocations, reveal the coming delay, or recommend a particular customer agreement. Wait before helping; record the exact point, time and kind of help. If blocked for 90 seconds, ask “What are you trying to do?” before directing attention. Mark facilitator help separately from app guidance. Stop on access/data loss, uncontrolled recording or unexpected real-world action. Do not use voice in this round: hosted voice remains unverified. Listed questions and manual controls remain available when the shared AI allowance is exhausted; record this as a limitation rather than hiding it.

Observe: finding the goal; confirmed versus expected stock; drafting/saving; interpreting a failed review; understanding that an offer is not agreement; revising after delay; locating handoff; reopening the saved result. For willing participants with time, try the changed-condition replay separately.

Severity: blocker = cannot continue; major = wrong decision due to interface confusion; minor = delay without changing decision; suggestion = preference.

## Five neutral follow-up questions

1. What job did you think you were doing?
2. Which decision was hardest, and why?
3. What changed after the supplier update?
4. What would the next person still need to know from your handoff?
5. When would you use this, if at all? What would you use instead?

Ask ease of completing the task from 1 (very difficult) to 7 (very easy), then ask why. Do not lead with “Was the AI useful?” or request a positive testimonial.

## Practitioner review (20 minutes)

Provide `docs/career/MISSION-SPEC.md` and a blank scenario first. Ask which promises, cutoffs, stock assumptions, negotiation options and handoff fields are implausible or missing; which local practice differs; what a novice might wrongly learn; and what minimal change would improve realism. Record proposed changes and rationale, not merely a numeric rating. Any new reference plan must be reviewed against domain rules before becoming a recommended answer.

## Decision gates

- Any access/data-loss blocker: repair before further recruitment.
- Two learners misunderstand the same control: prioritize copy/layout correction and rerun that task with fresh participants.
- Practitioner flags an unsafe or unrealistic core assumption: revise/version the scenario before broader pilot.
- Report completion as counts (e.g. 3/5), with assistance and sample limitations. Never invent quotes or convert task completion into learning gains.
