# R3 storyboard — design a study hub that works

Type: design explanation. Revision 3. Planned frames for the energy lab. These are not screenshots or claims of an implemented UI. The running `/mission-preview` is documented in the [historical API storyboard](API-PROTOTYPE-STORYBOARD.md).

## 1. A concrete invitation

Title: “Keep the study hub running.” A small original illustration shows a lamp, router, fan, laptop and battery. The brief states four hours, 240 Wh usable energy, 120 W limit and minimum service times. One primary action opens the timeline. No essay or long onboarding form.

## 2. A plan with visible consequences

Four device rows and four hour columns form the editable schedule. A learner runs the initial all-on plan. Result: 416 Wh requested, 104 W peak, services scheduled. The energy budget fails while the power limit passes. The visual and accessible table use the same result receipt.

## 3. The right experiment at the right time

An unobtrusive coach card points to the failed energy constraint and offers a short duration comparison. The learner selects a prediction or manipulates a time control, then sees the modelled effect. A source is available. This is live Bedrock-selected guidance only when the actual provider ran; authored fallback is visibly identified.

## 4. Conversation changes the artifact

The learner uses a specific spoken command to shorten a device period. The recognized command appears, followed by a committed edit and undo. Another direct edit completes a valid plan. Running it produces 188 Wh and 104 W for the example layout. The coach does not declare proficiency.

## 5. Changed conditions reveal the concept

B has 360 Wh but an 80 W power ceiling. The candidate fits energy and fails power at 104 W. The learner moves charging/fan periods apart. A valid plan reaches 312 Wh with an 80 W peak. The screen labels independent versus assisted work; the voice interface can translate explicit input without suggesting the answer.

## 6. Continuity is inspectable

Pause and reopen an unfinished attempt. The exact schedule and last result return. A short message identifies the unresolved constraint from a saved receipt. No claim of a delayed learning effect comes from a reload. C offers a distinct review with actual elapsed time shown.

## 7. A useful tutor handoff

An evidence packet shows the artifact, relevant runs, help, changed-task result and remaining uncertainty. A tutor can inspect what led to a result. The learner previews and explicitly chooses sharing. The report never says “certified” or silently exposes a full voice transcript.

## Visual priorities

The artifact occupies the largest area. Strong differences in energy and instantaneous power are readable without animation. Use purposeful short transitions for moving a load and comparing curves; provide a reduced-motion equivalent. Coach text stays concise and close to the evidence it references. Service/provider metadata belongs in an expandable demo/technical information area.

Story acceptance: a novice can explain what changed between A and B, why the original candidate failed, and what the report establishes. The complete path works using a keyboard without speech.
