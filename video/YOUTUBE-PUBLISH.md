## Description

Practice a fictional operations shift before your first job. LearnSprint lets learners investigate source facts, plan deliveries, respond to a supplier delay, record a customer agreement and save a handoff of their decisions.

Created by Nguyễn Xuân Hải.

Try the demo: https://d2g4a2ezl5lw7r.cloudfront.net/career
Source code (MIT): https://github.com/xuanhai0913/LearnSprint

Built with React, TypeScript, NestJS and SQLite, hosted through Amazon CloudFront and Amazon EC2. Amazon Bedrock Nova 2 Lite interprets bounded requests; learners review and explicitly apply proposed changes.

This video combines real recordings of the deployed app with explanatory animated diagrams. Narration is synthetic male English; English and Vietnamese subtitles are included.

This is an independent hackathon prototype and an Alexa+ style web simulation, not a native Alexa integration. Hosted voice interaction remains a prototype. Practitioner review and a learner pilot are next steps; no certification or employment outcome is claimed.

AWS service icons are from the official AWS Architecture Icons library and identify the services used. AWS trademarks belong to Amazon.
