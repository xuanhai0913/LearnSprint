# V2 editorial notes

## Editorial goal

Demonstrate why working through a simulated shift offers more than asking a chatbot for an answer: the learner inspects source facts, edits an operational board, handles a time-dependent shortage, records a customer agreement and confirms an AI-proposed change. The saved handoff and changed-condition replay make the decisions inspectable. No learning benefit or employability claim is inferred from this demonstration.

## Story arc

1. **Challenge:** three customers, eighty kits, a late delivery. Ask what the learner would promise.
2. **Audience:** students and first-time job seekers preparing for operations work.
3. **Flow:** discover → decide → adapt → reflect → replay.
4. **Evidence:** Alex distinguishes confirmed stock from expected stock.
5. **Plan:** allocate orders and inspect the rule-based review.
6. **Disruption:** explain the stock shortage at today's departure with an animated timeline.
7. **Commitment:** inspect Jordan's split-delivery offer and record the agreement.
8. **AI:** show the actual Nova 2 Lite proposal and explicit human Apply.
9. **Recovery:** move the remaining units to tomorrow and review the whole plan.
10. **Handoff:** preserve changes, source facts and assistance used.
11. **Replay:** changed stock/customer conditions, a fresh board and separate help history.
12. **Architecture:** React → CloudFront → private EC2/NestJS, with Bedrock and SQLite on EBS.
13. **Next:** practitioner review and a learner pilot; voice remains a prototype.

## Presentation decisions

- Male English narration: Wayne, Singapore locale. Natural English was prioritized over pretending this voice has a Vietnamese accent.
- English captions above Vietnamese translations, in a dedicated bottom band.
- Warm paper, forest green and orange match the application.
- Real recorded interactions remain the main demonstration; diagrams explain the scenario and system.
- Eased zooms emphasize facts, agreement and Apply; source footage is retimed to narration. This is an edited walkthrough, not an uninterrupted session.
- Short directional reveals connect chapters. Animated nodes/arrows explain the flow rather than introducing unrelated visual effects.
- No native Alexa integration, working hosted Sonic action, certification, pilot outcome or maximum judging score is claimed.
